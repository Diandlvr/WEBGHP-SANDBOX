Our Story

A Boutique
Urban Sanctuary

Nestled within the iconic Twist Tower — a contemporary architectural marvel known for its distinctive colors — Global Hotel Panama is part of the Global Companies Group, offering 60 beautifully appointed rooms and suites with breathtaking city skyline views.

Situated in the exclusive Obarrio district, the hotel places guests just minutes from Panama City's thriving financial center, surrounded by prestigious boutiques, restaurants, and the vibrant pulse of the capital.

What We Offer

World-Class Amenities

Rooftop Pool

An outdoor swimming pool and whirlpool on the 12th floor with spectacular panoramic views of the Panama City skyline.

Fitness Center

A state-of-the-art health club on the 12th floor featuring modern equipment and a private massage room.

Fine Dining

A sophisticated restaurant and wine bar at ground level offering diverse culinary experiences and elegant evening dining.

Business Center

Four fully equipped boardrooms plus a stylish event and meeting space on the 12th floor, ideal for corporate travelers.

Secure Parking

On-site private parking available for guests, with valet service ensuring convenience throughout your stay.

Free Wi-Fi

High-speed wireless internet access throughout all public areas and in every room, keeping you connected effortlessly.

Airport Transfer

Convenient airport transfer service available from Tocumen International Airport, just 20 minutes from the hotel.

24-hr Concierge
