Our Story

A Boutique
Urban Sanctuary

Nestled within the iconic Twist Tower — a contemporary architectural marvel known for its distinctive colors — Global Hotel Panama is part of the Global Companies Group, offering 60 beautifully appointed rooms and suites with breathtaking city skyline views.

Situated in the exclusive Obarrio district, the hotel places guests just minutes from Panama City's thriving financial center, surrounded by prestigious boutiques, restaurants, and the vibrant pulse of the capital.
